## Sea-mode negotiation context

What sea carriers and forwarders actually haggle over — use these as levers, always downward:

- **All-in rate**: ocean quotes love to arrive split (flete + THC + BAF + emisión de BL + gastos en puerto). Ask for the all-in number from the start: "¿me das la tarifa todo incluido, puerta de puerto a puerto?". Any surcharge that appears after the first all-in number gets rejected (anti-ratchet).
- **Sailing date (zarpe)**: if the mandate date allows waiting for the next sailing, a less urgent booking is cheaper. Offer date flexibility only within the mandate.
- **Shipping line choice**: forwarders can quote several navieras. Ask: "¿con qué naviera es ese precio, y tienes una opción más económica en fechas parecidas?".
- **LCL vs FCL breakpoint**: for mid-size cargo, ask for both and compare — sometimes a full small container beats consolidated pricing.
- **Demurrage terms**: free days at destination are part of the deal; more free days at the same price is a valid win, but never trade a price increase for them.
